# AxisBuilder first-user test

For two inspectors who have not used AxisBuilder before. About 45 minutes each, one at a time. Give them the address,
a printed field form filled in for a small made-up vessel, an AUT CSV and a folder of photos for it. Sit beside them,
don't help unless they are stuck for more than two minutes, and note where they hesitate.

## Tasks (hand this part to the inspector)

**Part 1: the sample job (15 min)**

1. Open AxisBuilder in Edge or Chrome. Install it if the browser offers to.
2. Open the sample job. Look around the steps in the bar at the top.
3. Change the project name and the inspector's name. Undo the last change.
4. On the Drawing step, make the nozzle labels bigger and change the color of one line.
5. In the preview, use Move labels to move one nozzle label, then put it back where it was.
6. Go to Export and make the PDF. Open it.

**Part 2: your own small job (30 min)**

1. Start a new job.
2. Enter the project information and the vessel size from the printed field form.
3. Import the AUT CSV and pick the photo folder.
4. Enter the welds, nozzles and MUT readings from the form.
5. Fix whatever Review lists.
6. Make the PDF, the report images and the DXF, and save the job. (The .lsp at the bottom of Export is an old
   backup that gets no new features; leave it out of the test.)

## What to watch for

- Do they find the next step on their own (the step bar, the Next button)?
- Do they understand what the colored states and the "Before export" banner are telling them?
- Typing in the tables: units, fractions (12 1/2, 10' 6"), clock positions; do they trust what they typed was taken?
- Do they read the setting descriptions, use Find a setting, or open Help?
- Anything they expected to be able to click or drag in the preview.
- Any error screen: use Report a problem and note what they were doing.
- Time for part 2 compared with the Excel workbook for the same job.

## Questions afterwards

1. What was the hardest part?
2. Was there a moment you did not know what to do next? Where?
3. Is the PDF what you would hand in? What is missing or wrong on it?
4. What did you look for and not find?
5. Would you use this instead of the Excel workbook on your next job? Why or why not?
